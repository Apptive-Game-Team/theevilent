# Comic Authoring

Turn the requested story into readable sequential art. Preserve the user's latest
direction and the existing visual identity; a polished illustration is not enough
if the story, scale, reading order, or character intent has become unclear.

## Ground the task

Read these local sources before story or art work:

- [World](../../../content/arcane-casters/story/WORLD.md): current decisions,
  rejected premises and spoiler boundaries.
- [Characters](../../../content/arcane-casters/story/CHARACTERS.md): stable IDs,
  current names, appearance, behavior and ending states.
- [Dialogue](../../../content/arcane-casters/story/DIALOGUE.md): voices,
  relationships, nonverbal creatures and card use.
- [Comic style](../../../content/arcane-casters/story/COMIC-STYLE.md): rendering,
  paneling and the roles of approved references.

For an existing episode, inspect its PLAN.md and current production status before
choosing images or prompts. Old art and numbered versions can be superseded.
Use the latest human instruction over stale status files; continuing the comic
after map work authorizes resuming it without another approval checkpoint.

Keep detailed canon in those documents, not duplicated inside this skill.
Generated details do not establish new lore, identities, powers or approval.

## Plan and script

For dialogue, episode structure or panel work, read
[Page and dialogue planning](references/page-planning.md).

- Record the scene's starting situation, what each character wants, the meaningful
  actions/reactions, and the ending change or next question.
- Allocate space by importance and reading rhythm. Do not impose a universal
  page count, panel quota, cliffhanger, conflict size, or layout from a past episode.
- Write speech alongside visible behavior. Keep character voices distinct,
  remove redundant explanations and preserve room for silent action.
- Before finished art, specify shots, focus, balloon order and space in a compact
  text layout or visual thumbnail appropriate to the request. Do not force a
  separate approval step when the user already requested the finished work.

## Direct the art

For page illustration, comic backgrounds or setting maps, read
[Art direction and continuity](references/art-direction.md).

Inspect the actual local references, not only filenames or old prompts. Distinguish
rendering, identity, geography and layout roles before generation. Keep characters
and scenery in the same ink/color vocabulary.

Before choosing a background, use the current map to check the scene location,
camera position and viewing direction, travel direction, distance and occlusion.
Include landmarks only when that shot can actually see them. Do not add the
mage tower as a recurring background marker or move it to make it visible.
Follow the visibility and movement checks in the art-direction reference.

Use the available image-generation workflow for raster art. If the imagegen skill
is available, follow it; do not silently substitute code graphics or another image
service. Save exact prompts and project-bound outputs in the story directory.
Keep selected artwork and discarded explorations distinguishable and preserve
approved references.

## Review and continue

At normal reading size, check:

- The page adds an action, relationship change, observation, or useful beat.
- Faces, physical cards, costumes and nonverbal acting follow the current sources.
- Panels and balloons read in order; tails identify the right speakers.
- Background scale, camera, ink and color match the intended scene and style.
- Visible landmarks and travel direction agree with the map; absent or obscured
  landmarks have not been inserted for visual continuity.
- No reference has imported rejected buildings, speaking creatures or spoilers.

Correct observable failures rather than accumulating new universal rules.
When the user steers the work, update affected scripts/prompts and status, then
continue the authorized scope. Report exact page coverage and remaining work;
never describe partial pages as a finished episode.

Keep source art, maps and author spoilers outside served assets. Drawing an
episode does not authorize publication, deployment, or posting elsewhere.

## Sources and maintenance

The [source-backed craft notes](../../../content/arcane-casters/story/references/comic-writing-notes-20261029.md)
contain the articles read, their scope, and applications to this project.
Use those notes when revisiting composition or dialogue; do not re-search every
ordinary page. If new research is requested, read primary creator/official sources,
retain links, and distinguish advice from the project's own creative decisions.

Update this skill for reusable production lessons. Update world/profile/style
documents for changing story facts. Do not turn one scene's camera, exact lines,
map shape, panel count, or approval status into a permanent rule.
